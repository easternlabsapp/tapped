# Third-party data and attribution

This repository's source code is licensed under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The datasets below are bundled into the backend and keep their own terms.

## Drinking-water violation records

- **Source:** US Environmental Protection Agency, Safe Drinking Water Information System (SDWIS). US government works are in the public domain.
- **Distribution:** records are retrieved through [WaterViolations.org](https://waterviolations.org) (JSON API and per-state CSV downloads). The site requests a link back when its data is used.
- **Bundled in:** `backend/src/data/systemIndex.ts` (a derived index of water systems and violation summaries, generated by `backend/scripts/build-system-index.mjs`).

## Postal-code locations

- **Source:** [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) postal code data, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
- **Bundled in:** `backend/src/data/zipPlaces.ts` (generated by `backend/scripts/build-zip-places.mjs`).
- **Required attribution** (to be shown in the application's credits screen): "Postal code data from GeoNames (geonames.org), CC BY 4.0."

## Disclaimer

The data is provided for general information about regulatory compliance history. It is not a measurement of current water quality.
